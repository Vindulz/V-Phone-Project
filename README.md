# V-Phone

V-Phone is a Website that serves as a e-commerce hub for all needs about tech, specifically phones! It offers a wide range of choices and products are categorized into 3 main sections (Gaming, Photography, and Budget). Each category has its own price range, specifications and models.

## FEATURES OF THE APP

### USERS

Users are able to Register a new account or Login if they already have one. Users will be able to **Browse**,**Add To Cart**,**And Checkout**. Users will also be able to see their purchase history and all of the past orders that they have made.

### ADMIN

Admins have a special privelege, where they can **Add**, **Edit**, **or Delete** products from the lineup. They are also able to manage registered users aswell as see total revenue generated by placed orders, and the stocks of each item that are on sale.
